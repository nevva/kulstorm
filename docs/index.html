Kulstorm

Run. Shoot. Survive.

Enemies come in three lanes and bunch up at the front. Drag sideways to steer, swipe up to jump and down to duck. Your toughest squad member leads, and if you run straight into a wall they headbutt it apart. Shoot the walls to pieces: each wall gives a new squad member with their own ability and health, or a perk for the whole squad. Chests hold power-ups and items: unlock more in the Armory to fill them with better loot.
